# The Wolfpack Journal

> A public, sanitized record of building the Wolfpack — Felipe Harms's personal system of AI agents.

This repository documents the construction of the Wolfpack, a personal system of AI agents in operation since February 2026. The content is narrative, sanitized, and limited to what is public: what was decided, what changed, and what stayed as a lesson. It is not operational documentation, a marketing page, or a daily log. Each entry records a verifiable milestone — something that shifted how we think, work, or decide — not a calendar day.

If you want to know **what the Wolfpack is** in the project's own words, start with [`manifesto.en.md`](manifesto.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`manifesto.md`](manifesto.md)). For **who participates**, head to [`a-matilha.en.md`](a-matilha.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`a-matilha.md`](a-matilha.md)). For **the bird's-eye view**, [`linha-do-tempo.en.md`](linha-do-tempo.en.md) is the entry (and in Portuguese at [`linha-do-tempo.md`](linha-do-tempo.md)). For the detailed stories, step into [`diario/`](diario/).

## How to read this journal

Each entry follows the same shape:

```
context → decision → change → public evidence → lesson
```

The template lives at [`templates/entrada.en.md`](templates/entrada.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`templates/entrada.md`](templates/entrada.md)). Mistakes and abandoned decisions show up explicitly in [`falhas-e-licoes/README.en.md`](falhas-e-licoes/README.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`falhas-e-licoes/README.md`](falhas-e-licoes/README.md)). Decisions that survived the test of time live in [`decisoes/README.en.md`](decisoes/README.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`decisoes/README.md`](decisoes/README.md)).

## What you will **not** find here

This repository is public. Some things do not belong in it: personal routine, customer data, infrastructure, secrets, internal paths, dashboard screenshots, attack details. The full policy is at [`docs/privacidade-red-lines.md`](docs/privacidade-red-lines.md).

If you came here looking for a technical detail — a port, an IP, a flow, a configuration — this is probably the wrong place. The journal is about thinking and decision-making, not engineering.

## On names and roles

The names Sif, Artorias, Ciaran, Ornstein, and Seath are **identity and convention** for the system's roles. They describe neither real people nor autonomous agents with lived experience. Felipe owns strategic direction and the material sign-off on every decision with external, destructive, irreversible, or production-critical consequences. The other names are specialized components of the system, with no independent human agency. Details in [`a-matilha.en.md`](a-matilha.en.md) (and in Portuguese at [`a-matilha.md`](a-matilha.md)).
