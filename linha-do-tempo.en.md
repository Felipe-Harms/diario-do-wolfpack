# Timeline

> The five milestones that inaugurate this journal.

This timeline is the macro picture. Each item has a detailed entry under `diario/AAAA/MM/`.

> **Note on origin.** The five records below were written as **sanitized retrospectives** from public memory and verifiable milestones. They are not real-time transcripts. Where concrete public evidence is not available, the item is declared as a **decision** or **retrospective account**, not as a fact verifiable by public link. The detailed entries under `diario/` repeat this notice explicitly.

## 1. February 2026 — Foundation

The project begins. Felipe starts using the system as a daily productivity assistant. Initial scope: calendar, information lookup, reminders, drafts. Little infrastructure, plenty of iteration.

See detail at [`diario/2026/02/fundacao.md`](diario/2026/02/fundacao.md).

## 2. June 2026 — Gateway security cycle

A configuration incident takes the environment down for a few minutes. Root cause: an under-reviewed addition to a configuration file. Takeaway: a convention for safe changes, validation before applying, and the first recorded appearance of failure by absence of verification.

See detail at [`diario/2026/06/seguranca-gateway.md`](diario/2026/06/seguranca-gateway.md).

## 3. July 2026 — Operational robustness

A cron stalls, an authentication expires without notice, a command is too long for the operating system. A whole month of small fixes. The perception: maturing is not only about adding features, it is about making what already exists stop breaking.

See detail at [`diario/2026/07/robustez-operacional.md`](diario/2026/07/robustez-operacional.md).

## 4. August 2026 — Adoption of the motto

*Esse quam videri.* The phrase is simple; the consequence is deep. Status must be distinguished from progress, and Felipe starts demanding this as routine. It is the starting point of the public journal.

See detail at [`diario/2026/08/lema-adotado.md`](diario/2026/08/lema-adotado.md).

## 5. August 2026 — Publication of this journal

The repository `diario-do-wolfpack` is published as MIT. It is the first public narrative piece of the Wolfpack. The criterion: write less, record better, and only publish what is in fact public.

See detail at [`diario/2026/08/diario-publico.md`](diario/2026/08/diario-publico.md).

---

Why five and not more? Because five was what fit into milestones that could be narrated publicly without exposing what is not. The rest stays in [`falhas-e-licoes/`](falhas-e-licoes/), in [`decisoes/`](decisoes/), and in private records. Future entries arrive through branch + PR, with mandatory human review before they go live.
