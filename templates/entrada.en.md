# Journal entry template

> Use this template for every new entry under `diario/AAAA/MM/<slug>.md`.

## Context

What was happening before. Not to justify an isolated fact — paint the picture so the reader understands why the thing matters.

## Decision

What was decided. In one or two sentences. Do not write more than that.

## Change

What changed after the decision. In behavior, in code, in habit. Not in aspiration.

## Public evidence

What someone outside can see as proof. Link, commit, file, public record. Evidence here must be verifiable without entering private spaces.

> **Honesty rule.** If there is no verifiable public evidence, the item must be declared explicitly as a **decision** or **retrospective account**, not as a fact verifiable by public link. Do not publish as evidence anything that depends on internal memory, private logs, internal screenshots, or non-public context.

## Lesson

What stayed. In one sentence. If the lesson needs three sentences, it is two lessons.

---

## Usage notes

- The date is the date of the event, not the date of publication.
- The `slug` is short `kebab-case` describing the milestone, not the day.
- The entry does not need to be long. It needs to be precise.
- Before publishing, three questions:
  1. Does this entry contradict any previous entry?
  2. Could it be shown to Felipe as proof of something?
  3. Can someone unfamiliar with the project read it and understand what changed?
