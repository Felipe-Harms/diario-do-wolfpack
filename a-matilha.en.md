# The pack

> The roles of the Wolfpack system.

The Wolfpack is composed of one human owner and a set of specialized components. This file presents the roles publicly and how they combine — no internal IDs, no paths, no topology, no credentials.

## Direction and approval

**Felipe Harms.** Owns strategic direction and material approval of every decision with external, destructive, irreversible, or production-critical consequences. Operational details that depend on private context are not covered here.

## System roles

The names below are **identity and convention** for the system's roles. They describe neither real people nor autonomous agents with lived experience; they are specialized components that operate under Felipe's direction.

- **Sif — coordination.** Consolidates, maintains the journal, manages the backlog, avoids duplicated work, and generates the report for Felipe.
- **Artorias — engineering.** Implements code, prepares repositories, and maintains the technical base.
- **Ciaran — research and validation.** Verifies plans, checks sources, and acts as reviewer.
- **Ornstein — operations.** Takes care of infrastructure, schedules, configuration, and authentication.
- **Seath — memory.** Consolidates learnings, recovers context, and organizes long-term memory.

## Composition principle

Each role exists because it covers something the others do not. When a role grows too large, it subdivides. When a role disappears, it is recorded in [`falhas-e-licoes/`](falhas-e-licoes/).

## What is **not** here

There are no internal counterparts, IDs, paths, credentials, models, providers, infrastructure, or schedules. That information is private by design. If you are reading this looking for topology, this repository is not for you.
