# Foundation — February 2026

> A sanitized retrospective. Where no public link exists, the item is declared as a **decision** or **retrospective account**, not as a verifiable fact.

## Context

In February 2026, the system begins being used as a daily productivity assistant. The initial scope is small: calendar, information retrieval, reminders, drafts.

There were no routines in place yet, no accumulated history. Only the capacity to pay attention to what was asked and try to answer usefully.

## Decision

Felipe decides to keep the system open to many kinds of task, avoiding rigid specialization. In exchange, the possibility of error is accepted before the style converges.

The rule adopted: every task must end in a verifiable output. No "ok, done" without delivering what was specifically requested.

## Change

Three points shift in how Felipe interacts with the system from this decision onward:

- Every completed task is recorded with its concrete output (file, link, reminder created).
- Ambiguous requests are bounced back as a question, instead of being guessed.
- Tasks that touch sensitive files now require explicit human review.

## Public evidence

- **Decision recorded retrospectively:** the milestone "foundation in February 2026" is declared in this publication, with no public link of origin attached to this entry.
- **Retrospective account:** the first public appearance of the "Wolfpack" name in public comments is said to have happened in March 2026, according to a reconstruction from public memory — with no public link attached to this entry.

## Lesson

Starting small, accepting error, and recording what was done are the preconditions of everything that came later — security, robustness, motto, journal. Without that foundation, nothing else fits.
