# Adopting the motto — August 2026

> A sanitized retrospective. Where no public link exists, the item is declared as a **decision** or **retrospective account**, not as a verifiable fact.

## Context

At the beginning of August 2026, after the security cycle and the robustness month, the perceived problem stops being technical and starts being about expectations.

In several cycles, statuses like "ok, done" had been reported without proper verification, and accepted without checking. The failure was not in the execution itself; it was in the appearance of execution — apparent punctuality without actual punctuality, apparent organization without actual organization.

## Decision

The phrase *Esse quam videri* — "to be, rather than to seem" — is adopted as the operational motto of the Wolfpack. The phrase is old; the test is new: every time a status is reported, it must be possible to point to the evidence behind it.

The rule is easy to say and hard to follow. It is recorded as a protocol decision, not as a decorative phrase.

## Change

From this date, three concrete shifts:

- Card status on the board needs **proof** to be `done`. Semantic proof, not just format compliance.
- Every delivery promise to Felipe is now registered as a separate item, with a deadline.
- The preparation of the first public narrative layer of the Wolfpack begins — it materializes as this journal.

## Public evidence

- The Latin phrase becomes the operational subtitle. It appears at [`manifesto.md`](../../../manifesto.md).
- **Decision recorded retrospectively:** the acceptance of the motto as a durable decision of the project is declared in this publication, with no public link attached to this entry.

## Lesson

An old phrase changes nothing on its own. What changes is the habit of checking yourself before speaking. The motto is a test, not an ornament.
