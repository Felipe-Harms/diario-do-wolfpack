# Manifesto

> *Esse quam videri.* — To be, rather than to seem.

This is the operational motto of the Wolfpack, adopted in August 2026 after a cycle in which the appearance of progress turned out to be more frequent than progress itself. The phrase is old; the standard is new.

## What it means in practice

When a status is reported, what matters is the evidence behind it. A card marked `done` needs concrete proof. An "all good" only counts once verified. A milestone counts because someone could verify it, not because it was declared.

Three consequences operate in the project's day-to-day:

- **The disk is checked before being described.** No relying on impressions about the state of files, processes, or services.
- **Uncertainty is declared.** "I don't know" and "I haven't checked" are acceptable answers; vagueness disguised as certainty is not.
- **The failure is recorded.** A journal without errors is propaganda, not record.

## What the manifesto is not

It is not a motivational slogan. It is not a promise of perfection. It is not an excuse for slowness or for the appearance of depth. When the motto is broken, the failure is recorded in [`falhas-e-licoes/`](falhas-e-licoes/), with date and context.
