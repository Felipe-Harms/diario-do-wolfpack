# Failures and lessons

> When something fails, it gets recorded. When something is learned, it also gets recorded.

This folder is the public record of what went wrong and what remained as a lesson. There is no shame here — there is traceability. The motto *Esse quam videri* demands that we confuse appearance with reality less often; this folder is the concrete proof that the motto is taken seriously.

## What belongs here

- Failures that affected delivery, deadline, operation, or security.
- Decision failures in which the chosen path ended up being discarded.
- Near-misses that would have been severe if the timing had been different.

## What **does not** belong here

- Infrastructure details that expose topology.
- Names of third parties, clients, or internal systems.
- Screenshots, messages, or private communications.

## How a failure is recorded

Each entry follows the same format:

- **What happened.** In one sentence.
- **Why it happened.** Root cause, without blaming a tool or a third party.
- **What changed.** What was different afterwards.
- **Lesson.** In one sentence.

The first documented failure in the Wolfpack is the gateway security cycle, at [`diario/2026/06/seguranca-gateway.md`](../diario/2026/06/seguranca-gateway.md). It is what gives rise to the set of rules that still hold today.
