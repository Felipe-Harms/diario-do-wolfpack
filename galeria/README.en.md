# Gallery

> Public images and visuals of the Wolfpack.

This folder is empty in this V1. It was created to receive, in the future, public visuals of the Wolfpack — diagrams, photos of gatherings, mockups, visual records of milestones.

## Entry policy

Only material that satisfies three criteria enters:

- **Public.** It does not carry private information about people, infrastructure, operations, or third parties.
- **Sanitized.** It does not show tokens, paths, internal IDs, panel screenshots, or personal data.
- **Authorized.** The rights holder permits publication. If there are faces, there is authorization.

## What **does not** enter

- Screenshots of dashboards, logs, alerts, or metrics.
- Photos of internal system screens.
- Images with personally identifiable data.
- Any asset that exposes topology, network, credentials, or environment.

## How to contribute

Open a branch, attach the asset, open a PR. Human review is mandatory before merging.
