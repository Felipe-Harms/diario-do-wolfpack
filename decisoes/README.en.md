# Decisions

> Protocol decisions of the Wolfpack that survive time.

This folder holds durable decisions. It is not a meeting log. It is not a changelog. It is a durable record of why the Wolfpack is the way it is.

## What belongs here

- Protocol decisions that affect how the Wolfpack operates.
- Process decisions that will guide more than a single delivery.
- Explicit scope decisions (what is and what is not the Wolfpack's responsibility).

## What **does not** belong here

- One-off decisions about a specific task.
- Operational decisions that depend on private infrastructure.
- Decisions involving clients, real cases, or third-party data.

## How a decision is recorded

Each decision here follows the same format:

- **Context.** What motivated the decision.
- **Decision.** What was settled.
- **Consequence.** What changes in behavior from now on.
- **When to revisit.** Under what conditions the decision stops holding.

The first decision recorded in this repository is the adoption of the motto *Esse quam videri*, covered at [`diario/2026/08/lema-adotado.md`](../diario/2026/08/lema-adotado.md).
